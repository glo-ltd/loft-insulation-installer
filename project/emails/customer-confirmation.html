The benefits

One of the smartest improvements you can make.

Around 25% of your home's heat can escape through an uninsulated roof. Here's what a proper layer of sustainable insulation gives you back:

Cut your energy bills

By keeping heat inside, insulation reduces how hard your heating works, saving you up to £315 a year on energy bills.

A warmer home in winter

No more overworking the heating to stay comfortable. A well-insulated loft holds warmth where you need it.

A cooler home in summer

The right amount of insulation ventilates the roof space and keeps your home cooler when it's hot outside.

Less mould & condensation

Breathable materials help regulate humidity, limiting the condensation that leads to mould.

A smaller carbon footprint

Less wasted energy means fewer carbon emissions: a real, lasting reduction, year after year.

Adds value to your home

A fully, properly insulated home is more efficient and more appealing to buyers, and can lift your property's value.

Saving of up to £315 a year is the estimate from Which? for a typical previously uninsulated home; actual savings vary by property.
